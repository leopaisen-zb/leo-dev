## Findings

No blocking or non-blocking production finding is confirmed in the reviewed scope.

Two initially suspicious cases were traced through the public entry points and are not findings:

- Recovery does not rely only on a private “latest Gate phase” check. Public replay classifies and validates Gate handoffs before admission, while the recovery binding independently requires one successful Controller Gate result, one matching settled terminal, a succeeded Run, and an active unreleased original lease. Thus an unresolved/unknown public Gate history is rejected before it can become a recovery context.
- Pending recovery is rebound using the current time, but the durable recovery time is the eventual committed event timestamp. A supported pending batch can therefore finish only after the original lease is expired and only with the exact prepared payload/ID. A manually invented pending operation is rejected by exact binding/provenance checks.
